# Elevator pitch

For emails, conversations, and anyone who asks what Artists Are Jerks is. Written September 25, 2026, from the About page's third paragraph.

> Most artists dream of gallery representation, and it's worth reaching for. But getting there takes years, and in the meantime you need to be seen. There are far more places to be seen than the art world admits: the café, the brewpub, the bookstore, the artist's own studio. Local musicians know this. You play the bar down the street while you work toward the record deal, and you keep playing it after. Artists Are Jerks is a calendar of where to see real art by local artists, in person, this week. It doesn't replace a gallery. It helps you get there, and it keeps working once you do.

Keep it in step with `src/pages/about.astro`. The point Alan cares about: galleries are still the goal. The site helps artists get seen on the way there and after.
